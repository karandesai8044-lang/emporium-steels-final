Company Overview

A Pan India Supplier of Stainless & Mild Steel

Emporium Steels is a leading importer, stockist and supplier of all types of Ferrous & Non-Ferrous Metals, based in Rakhiyal, Ahmedabad, Gujarat. We are manufacturers of Stainless Steel Pipes in all grades, shapes and sizes, and stock SS, MS & CS pipes, tubes, sheets, plates, flanges, fittings, valves, bars, rods, angles, circles and fasteners — along with Copper, Brass and Aluminium materials.

Since establishment in 2022, we have served clients in chemicals, fertilisers, petrochemicals, nuclear power, thermal power, cement, paper and many other industries on a repeat-order basis, with manufacturer & IBR test certification under third-party inspection.
